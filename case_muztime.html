Case Study 02 · In Development · iOS Mobile

MuzTime

Train in rhythm — a workout interval timer that stays locked to Spotify playback. The timer pauses when the music pauses, volume ducks by interval, and the next track is chosen by where your heart rate is headed.

Role Product Designer · Developer Tools Figma · Spotify API · Python

01 — The Problem

Two Apps That Don't Talk

Interval timers and music apps have never actually talked to each other — so the workout and the soundtrack fight instead of moving together.

What's Broken Today

Music keeps running through rest periods. Track intros get skipped mid-warm-up or, worse, don't. The two apps have no idea the other exists.

What MuzTime Does Instead

One integrated session — the timer and Spotify respond to each other automatically, with just enough manual control (ducking, playlist choice) to feel personal.

02 — Core Mechanics

Three Systems in Lockstep

Three systems keep the timer and the music in lockstep, without asking the user to babysit either one.

A

Linked Pause

The timer pauses the instant Spotify pauses, in both work and rest intervals — no separate controls mid-set.

B

Intro Skip

Spotify's Audio Analysis API detects quiet intros and skips them, so every round starts already on the beat.

C

Energy Queue

A two-tier AI queue sequences tracks from your own playlist by energy arc, with Spotify Recommendations as a labeled fallback.

04 — Locked Decisions

What Was Decided, and Why

01

Digital countdown over ring timer — a large bold number reads faster mid-set than an arc does. 80px on Home, 96px on Active.

02

Spotify Connect carries MuzTime's own accent — not Spotify green. The app needed its own identity from screen one.

03

Active track lives in a bottom sheet — playlist name, Spotify deep link, and current track, without covering the countdown.

04

Workout review as a one-line pill — the summary earns a glance, not a screen.

05

History groups by playlist — one tappable header drills into a list; AI-recommended tracks are flagged distinctly, never blended in unlabeled.

05 — Where It Stands

Seven Screens, Backend in Progress

Seven screens designed and decided. Backend build against the Spotify Web API is in progress — Python, learned largely on this project.
