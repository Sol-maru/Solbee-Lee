Case Study 03 · Personal Project · macOS AI Assistant

Desktop Agent

Two desktop companions built side by side: 비나리, a conversational AI thinking partner, and a jellyfish desktop pet that has no AI at all.

Timeline Aug 17, 2026 – Present Tools Claude · ChatGPT · Piskel

01 — Overview

Binari (비나리) is a macOS AI desktop assistant designed to support spontaneous idea development through Socratic conversation. Rather than requiring users to navigate to a separate application, Binari aims to provide an accessible thinking partner that helps capture unfinished thoughts, explore ideas, and organize them into actionable directions.

Alongside Binari, I am developing a separate jellyfish desktop pet focused on ambient interaction and character animation. Unlike Binari, the jellyfish does not use an LLM. Instead, it responds through predefined interactions and animated states, exploring how a small desktop character can create a sense of presence without demanding attention.

Although both projects explore companionship within the desktop environment, they approach it from different perspectives: Binari focuses on conversational assistance, while the jellyfish explores ambient presence and playful interaction.

02 — Goal

Design an ambient desktop assistant that helps users capture ideas and develop their reasoning with minimal interruption to their existing workflow. The experience centers on three principles:

  • Immediate accessibility: Make conversational assistance available through a single interaction without navigating away from the current workspace.
  • Thoughtful guidance: Use Socratic questioning to help users elaborate on their ideas rather than simply generating finished answers.
  • Unobtrusive presence: Create a companion that remains visually engaging while respecting the user's attention and autonomy.

The long-term goal is to develop a lightweight environment where thoughts can be recorded, revisited, and progressively transformed into creative work.

04 — Jellyfish

A desktop pet with no LLM behind it. It responds through predefined interactions and animated states, so everything it does is drawn and designed by hand. It is still unnamed. Its motions are modeled on the behaviors of Comnyang, an existing pixel-cat desktop app, which I use as a reference for what a desktop pet can do. The jellyfish has its own body and its own way of moving.
